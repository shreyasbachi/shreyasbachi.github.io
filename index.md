## About Me

I'm a Master's student in Machine Learning at **Carnegie Mellon University**, where I focus on efficient and reliable machine learning systems. I completed my B.S. in Informatics at Arizona State University (GPA 3.95), where I spent two years as an undergraduate researcher across reinforcement learning, model compression, and sustainable urban computing.

My work sits at the intersection of state-of-the-art models and real-world deployment constraints. Recent projects include compressing 100K-step reinforcement learning runs into tiny synthetic datasets, profiling energy bottlenecks in Mixture-of-Experts language models, generative AI for transportation planning, and optimizing neural networks for edge devices like the Jetson Nano.
